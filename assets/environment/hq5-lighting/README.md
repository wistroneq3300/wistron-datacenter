`manifest.json` records source/download/metadata URLs, authors, license, bytes, resolution, official MD5 and locally computed SHA-256. Download sizes and MD5 were checked; all four files were fully decoded by the project's RGBELoader on CPU. These checks do not constitute GPU, PMREM, visual or performance acceptance.

Use as generic illumination and reflections. These are **not** panoramas or measured lighting from Wistron's Xingshan Road site. Do not label the visible foreign environments as an actual Taipei streetscape. Load only the selected environment and dispose superseded resources as appropriate; do not assume all four need to remain resident.

No press photography, Poly Haven website previews, screenshots, logos or source-page content are redistributed in this directory. Research evidence and remaining limits are documented in `docs/phase5a/REFERENCES.md`.
